      <h3>Electronics, Arduino &amp; Robotics for Rural Students</h3>
      <p>Hands-on electronics, Arduino and robotics for 40 students from classes 9 to 11, from a remote rural area with little access to technology. We started by linking textbook physics like Ohm's law to real circuits. Most of the time went to Arduino and AlphaBot robots, where the students wrote their own logic to make the robots move, trace shapes and react to conditions. I also visited the Palghar Fab Lab and the Govardhan Skill Centre.</p>

Rather than one technology, the goal was to teach them how to learn new ones, working with microcontrollers, sensors, image processing and NLP. We also ran a fast-paced 3-hour session at Sanctorum School, Sopore, where students wrote movement and conditional logic to make AlphaBot2 robots trace shapes.</p>
